## The vision in one paragraph

A 30–50 hour survival RPG set in a cold frontier kingdom, in the spirit of Skyrim and Dark Souls but its own
thing. You play one of ten races, each with its own opening. The story runs on three layers at once: who you
are, a realm tearing itself apart in a succession war during an endless winter, and the slow waking of the
Titans, colossal beings of living stone sleeping under the land. Guilds, wars and choices close doors as well
as open them, so no two runs are alike, and the ending you reach (good, neutral, bad, or one of several
strange variants, including becoming the Rat King) reflects the whole road that led there.

## Decisions so far

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Length of one run | 30–50 hours, level cap 80 |
| Acts | I: levels 1–25 · II: 25–40 · IIIa (shared): 40–55 · IIIb (path-specific): 55–70 · endgame: 70–80 |
| Point of no return | Around level 55, at the end of Act IIIa |
| Setting | A cold frontier kingdom, human-centred, dwarves and elves common, other races rarer |
| Story layers | Personal → the war → the mythic Titans |
| Titans | Kept; reimagined as colossal living stone, each fused with an element |
| Hunting | Region-based hunts, town bounties, regional bosses, and lore-gated legendary hunts you choose to Track. Regions have a starting level, not a ceiling: the story sends you back to old regions and hunting there stays worthwhile. |
| The sacred fires | Cradlefires: ancient fires that "rock the cradle" of the sleeping Titans (replaces the Crown of Names) |
| Titan names | The Titans keep Morrg and Gorren; the arena fighters with those names get new ones |
| Guild locations | The Collegium is one tower at Glassmere; the Iron Company and the Hearth Order are in most towns; the Undercity has cells everywhere, biggest under Brennhold, and the Iron Company's headquarters sits right on top of it on purpose (a late twist) |
| The personal mystery | No intro and no "why". You're nobody special until your first Titan teaches you a word. Factions argue Chosen, Bloodline or Made; the answer (you're a piece of the Ninth) is NG+ only |
| The stone-cost | Story only. Turning to stone changes how you're described and how people react, never your stats |
| Secret early ending | A House in Emberwick: a few odd jobs for the innkeeper, then a 500-gold cottage that ends the game on the spot |
| Guilds | Four, with rivalries: joining one closes its rival, so nobody becomes master of everything |
| Political sides | The Regency, the Free Holds, the royal Claimant (backed by the Hearth Order and dwarven gold), plus staying Unsworn |
| Exotic factions | The Grove (druids and beastkin) and the Dwarven Holds of Aegis |
| Endings | Good, neutral and bad, plus three variants (the Rat King, the Lich, the Dragon's Pact), a secret early ending available from the start, and special endings later |
| Rat King | Definitely in the game, important to the Undercity, and an ending of his own |
| Player origin | Race-specific openings that converge into a shared Act I |
| NG+ and meeting your old self | Wanted, but later |
