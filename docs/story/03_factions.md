# Factions

Two kinds, as in classic RPGs: **guilds** you join for their questlines and secret stock, and **political
sides** you choose between in the war. Plus two **exotic factions** with a very different way of life.

## Guilds

Each guild has a questline, ranks, a hall, and a members-only shop with gear and spells you can't buy anywhere
else.

| Guild | Classic role | Who they are | Members-only stock |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Iron Company** | Fighters | A mercenary company that grew into an institution: hired swords and part-time city watch. Contracts, the arena in Brennhold, honour among sellswords. | Masterwork weapons and heavy armor; later, martial techniques |
| **The Collegium Arcana** | Mages | The realm's only sanctioned school of magic, in one great tower on the frozen lake at Glassmere. Elves, gnomes and human scholars. | The Collegium spells, staves and robes (today's magic shop becomes members-only) |
| **The Undercity** | Thieves | Smugglers, fences and cutpurses in the tunnels under Brennhold, answering to a master they call the Rat King. | Poisons, lockpicks, stealth gear, stolen goods at a discount |
| **The Hearth Order** | The Church | Keepers of the Cradlefires: healers, priests and inquisitors who hunt the undead and distrust mages. | Holy weapons, wards and healing; the best anti-undead gear |

### Where each guild lives

| Guild | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **The Collegium Arcana** | One place only: the great tower at Glassmere | A huge, ancient tower on the frozen lake. It never moves and they never build another. Not secret, but closed: you go to Glassmere or you don't deal with the Collegium. |
| **The Iron Company** | Chapterhouses in most towns | A fighters' guild that works like hired-gun police: town contracts, guard work, the arena. The same company everywhere, with a local captain in each chapterhouse. Its headquarters and arena are in Brennhold, directly above the Undercity's heart (see the twist below). |
| **The Undercity** | Cells under most towns; by far the biggest under Brennhold | Smugglers and fences everywhere, but the real Undercity, and the Rat King, is beneath the capital. |
| **The Hearth Order** | A shrine in every town, a Cradlefire on every hill | The church is everywhere; its seat is the Great Cradlefire in Brennhold. |

**What happens to today's quests.** The arena fighters (Ziggy, Jarvask, Yjurgen and the rest) become Iron
Company members. The Mages Academy becomes the Collegium at Glassmere. The tavern becomes the inn in each town
(Emberwick's first). The two fighters named Morrg and Gorren need new names, since those names now belong to
Titans.

### The Brennhold twist (secret until late)

The Iron Company's headquarters and arena sit in Brennhold, on top of the biggest Undercity in the realm. On the
surface that's the rivalry at its sharpest: the realm's hired law and its thieves, one floor apart.

The truth, kept until the top of either guild: **the Iron Company works for the Rat King.** Generations ago a
Company captain made a bargain in the tunnels. The Company gets coin, secrets and a city that needs policing;
the Rat King gets a police force that only ever arrests his competition. The rank and file on both sides
believe the rivalry is real, and fight and die over it. Only the very top knows.

- **Clues, for players who look.** The Company raids Undercity cells in every town except Brennhold. The arena's
  drains run straight down into the tunnels. Nobody ever poisons the rats in the chapterhouse cellars. The
  guildmaster is never seen eating.
- **The reveal.** Reaching the top of the Iron Company leads you down a drain to meet your real employer.
  Reaching the top of the Undercity shows you the Company's ledgers in the Rat King's hall. Players in neither
  guild can stumble onto it late in the Undercity arc.
- **The rivalry rule still holds.** Joining one still closes the other, all game. The twist is that it never
  meant what it looked like. Iron Company players can reach the Rat King ending by taking over the bargain;
  Undercity players by climbing to the throne.
- **Tone.** It's the joke and the horror at once: the hired police were standing on the Rat King the whole time.

### Rivalries: you can't be the best at everything

Joining a guild closes its rival. There are two rivalries:

| Rivalry | Why they hate each other |
|---|---|
| **Collegium ↔ Hearth Order** | Faith against arcane. The Order calls magic blasphemy; the Collegium calls the Order a protection racket with candles. |
| **Iron Company ↔ Undercity** | Law against shadow. The Company takes the city watch's contracts; the Undercity robs the city watch. |

So you can belong to **at most two guilds**, one from each rivalry, giving four combinations (for example Iron
Company and Collegium: the battlemage; Undercity and Hearth Order: the pious thief). The two rival guilds' halls
close to you, and their members treat you as an enemy.

**One master only.** You can rise to the top rank in only one guild. Becoming its leader makes your second guild
see you as an outsider, capped one rank below the top.

**Switching (rare).** A guild can be left by betrayal: a scene where you hand your guild over to its rival.
That costs everything you earned in the guild you betray. It is not a menu option.

## Political sides (the war)

The realm is split three ways over the throne. You can choose one (or none) by the end of Act II; the choice
reshapes Act III.

| Side | Leader (working name) | Their case | Their sins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Regency** | Lord Regent Aldous Varr | Order. A crown needs a strong hand in a crisis. Holds the capital and the army. | Taxes the starving to pay soldiers; burns villages that hide rebels |
| **The Free Holds** | Holdlord Maren Ostvik, "the Grey Wolf" | Self-rule. The highland lords feed the realm and want to keep their harvest. | Warlords in all but name; hangs collaborators; would split the realm |
| **The Claimant** | Lady Elisande, the late king's acknowledged daughter | Legitimacy. She has the blood and the Hearth Order's blessing, and the Holds' gold. | A puppet of priests and bankers, or a true queen? Nobody, including her, is sure |
| **Unsworn** | Nobody | Stay out of it. | Every side calls you a coward or a traitor, and the war happens without you |

## Exotic factions

| Faction | Way of life | What they want |
|---|---|---|
| **The Grove** | Druids and beastkin living by the old ways in the Ashwood and the north. No towns, no coin. | They believe the Titans *are* the land's seasons and that the long sleep was never natural. They want the Titans woken gently, not chained or killed. |
| **The Dwarven Holds of Aegis** | Proud, insular, mercantile clans inside the great mountain. Their halls, their law, their bank. | Keep the Holds safe and the money flowing. They're funding the Claimant, and the Worm is gnawing at their deepest halls. They don't know what the mountain really is. |

## How factions connect

This is where the interweaving comes from: guilds, politics and the Titans pull on each other.

| Faction | Leans toward in the war | What it wants done about the Titans |
|---|---|---|
| Iron Company | Whoever pays (currently the Regency) | Kill them. Every Titan is a contract. |
| Collegium Arcana | The Regency, which funds them | Study them, learn their names, bind them properly this time |
| Undercity | Everyone and no one | Nothing. A frozen kingdom is good for business. |
| Hearth Order | The Claimant | Relight every Cradlefire and burn whatever's unnatural (mages, the undead) |
| The Grove | The Free Holds (the highland forests) | Wake them gently and end the unnatural sleep |
| Dwarven Holds | The Claimant (with their gold) | Keep them asleep, especially whatever is under the mountain |

Every faction is partly right. That's what the endings argue about.

## Reputation

Each faction has standing from hostile to honoured, changed by quests, choices and race (see 02_races.md).
Standing changes prices, what's sold to you, who will talk to you, and which quests appear. Rival factions
react to each other: helping the Collegium costs you with the Hearth Order even before you join either.
